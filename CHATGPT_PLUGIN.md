# Unknown Unknowns for ChatGPT

**Stop AI from confidently helping you solve the wrong problem.**

Unknown Unknowns is an epistemic harness for product builders, researchers, developers, and decision-makers. It adds a deliberate challenge-and-probe layer before execution.

## What it does

The plugin can:

- separate observed facts, inferences, assumptions, and candidate blind spots;
- question whether the proposed solution, bottleneck, metric, or system boundary is wrong;
- surface omitted stakeholders, incentives, dependencies, and alternative explanations;
- distinguish uncertainty that can be resolved by reasoning, external evidence, or contact with reality;
- design small, reversible probes with observable outcomes, surprise conditions, and decision rules;
- run assumption audits, problem reframes, premortems, outside-view checks, and evidence-gap reviews.

## What it does not claim

A literal unknown unknown cannot be listed in advance. The plugin does not promise a complete risk inventory or guaranteed correctness. It helps expose the edges of the current model and creates conditions in which additional unknowns can become visible.

## Example prompts

- “Challenge this idea before I commit to building it.”
- “Find the assumptions and variables missing from my current frame.”
- “Design low-cost probes that could prove my decision wrong.”
- “Assume this launch failed in six months. Work backward to early signals and stop conditions.”
- “This task is simple and reversible. Note only the assumptions that matter, then solve it.”
